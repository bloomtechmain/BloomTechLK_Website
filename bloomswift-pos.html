Platform Features

Everything Your Business Needs
in One POS Platform

Built from the ground up with modern technology — BloomSwift POS handles every aspect of your business operations, from sales to inventory to reporting.

Inventory Management

Real-time stock tracking, reorder alerts, multi-warehouse support, and intelligent inventory control to eliminate stockouts.

Sales Analytics

Daily, weekly, monthly dashboards with top products, peak hours, cashier performance, and revenue trend analysis.

SMS Notifications

Automated SMS for order confirmations, loyalty rewards, low stock alerts, and daily sales summaries to owners and staff.

Role-Based Access

Granular permission controls for cashiers, supervisors, and admins. Protect sensitive data and operations with fine-grained roles.

Thermal Printing

One-click thermal receipt printing with customisable templates — your brand, logo, promotions, and footer on every receipt.

Multi-Location

Manage unlimited branches from one dashboard — centralised reporting, cross-location stock transfers, and unified customer data.

Cloud & Local Deploy

Runs on cloud servers or fully on-premises. Offline-capable so operations never stop, even when internet connectivity drops.

Real-Time Dashboard

Live business intelligence — sales velocity, top items, cashier productivity, and inventory levels refreshed every second.

Industries We Serve

Tailored POS for Every Vertical

BloomSwift POS is custom-built for each client — not a generic product. We understand the unique workflows, compliance, and realities of every sector we serve.

Retail

Barcode scanning, multi-variant products, loyalty programmes, and end-of-day reconciliation for single stores and multi-branch retail chains.

Restaurants & Cafes

Table management, kitchen display integration, split billing, course ordering, QR menus, and real-time void/discount controls for F&B.

Hospitality

Hotel POS with room charge posting, mini-bar billing, outlet management, folio settlement, and PMS integration for seamless guest experiences.

Pharmacy

Drug inventory with batch/expiry tracking, prescription logs, GST-compliant billing, and controlled substance reporting for pharmacies.

Supermarkets

High-throughput POS with weighing scale integration, bulk pricing, cashier lanes, and live stock sync across all departments and floors.

Enterprise & ERP

ERP-integrated POS with multi-currency, 10+ branch management, full audit trails, and custom reporting for large-scale operations.
