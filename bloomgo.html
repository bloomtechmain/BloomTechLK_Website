Platform Features

Everything the platform needs.
Built and ready.

BloomGo is a full-stack P2P car rental platform — from secure sign-in to post-journey photo evidence — built specifically for Sri Lanka.

Google Sign-In

One-click login via Google OAuth. No passwords. Every user is Google-verified before they can book or list.

List Your Vehicle

Owners add make, model, year, plate, price/day in LKR, location, photos, and availability calendar — live in minutes.

Smart Booking Engine

Date conflict detection, multi-day selection, and automatic total price calculation in LKR — no double bookings ever.

Booking Approval Flow

Owners approve or reject requests. Bookings move through: Pending → Confirmed → Completed or Rejected/Cancelled.

Pre-Journey Inspection

Mileage recording and photo upload before handover. Every inspection is stored per booking for full accountability.

Post-Journey Inspection

Mileage after return plus photo evidence. The owner is auto-notified the moment a renter completes the post-check.

In-App Notifications

Real-time alerts for owners: new booking requests, journey completions, status changes. Mark-as-read supported.

Owner & Renter Dashboards

Separate views: "My Rides" for renters, "Hire Outs" for owners — with full booking history and status tracking.

In-App Messaging

Dedicated chat room per booking for owner–renter coordination. Every message is tied to a specific booking for context.

Vehicle Media Gallery

Photos stored per vehicle and per journey check. Visual evidence for every handover — complete dispute protection.
