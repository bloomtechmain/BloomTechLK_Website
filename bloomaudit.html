Platform Features

Everything You Need to
Run Your Finances

10 powerful modules built for Sri Lankan businesses — click any card to learn more about what's included.

Advanced Accounting

Full double-entry ledgers & journals

Complete double-entry accounting system with automated ledgers, journals, trial balance, profit & loss, and balance sheet generation. Supports multiple chart-of-accounts structures for any business type.

Expense Tracking

Real-time capture across departments

Capture expenses in real time across categories and departments. Attach receipts, set approval workflows, and generate instant expense summaries by cost centre or project.

Bank Reconciliation

Manual reconciliation & transaction matching

Manual bank reconciliation tools with intelligent transaction matching, outstanding item tracking, and reconciliation reports for accurate month-end closing.

Advanced Analytics

Visual dashboards with trend charts

Visual dashboards showing income, expense, and profit trends over time. Drill-down reports, period comparisons, and exportable charts to share with stakeholders.

Payroll & EPF/ETF

Sri Lanka-compliant payroll automation

Fully compliant Sri Lankan payroll processing with automatic EPF (8% / 12%) and ETF (3%) calculations, salary slips, and government submission-ready reports.

Asset Management

Track assets, depreciation & write-offs

Maintain a complete fixed asset register with purchase cost, useful life, depreciation schedules (straight-line or reducing balance), and automated write-off journals.

Inventory Management

Stock tracking & reorder alerts

Real-time stock tracking across multiple locations, automatic reorder alerts, cost-of-goods-sold reporting, and inventory valuation using FIFO or weighted average methods.

Employee & Vendor Portal

Manage staff, vendors & access permissions

Centralised employee records, vendor contracts, purchase orders, and access permissions. Employees can access their own data; vendors can submit invoices through the portal.

Document Bank

Secure digital storage for all records

Encrypted cloud storage for invoices, contracts, bank statements, and financial records. Tag, search, and share documents securely with role-based access control.

Real-Time Notifications

Package expiry alerts & in-app updates

Automated alerts for subscription expiry, overdue invoices, low stock, payroll due dates, and system updates — delivered via email and in-app notifications.

Trust & Security

Your Financial Data is
Secured at Every Layer

Enterprise-grade security built into every part of the Bloom Audit platform — so you can focus on your finances, not your infrastructure.

JWT Authentication

Stateless JSON Web Token authentication with short-lived tokens and refresh rotation — every session is verified independently.

Bcrypt Password Encryption

All user passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage. Plain-text credentials are never written to disk or transmitted.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permissions ensure every user only sees and touches what they're authorised to — from data entry staff to directors.

Daily Package Monitoring

Automated daily checks on subscription status with advance expiry notifications so your team is never caught off guard.

Google OAuth 2.0

Sign in securely with your existing Google account — no extra password to remember, with full OAuth 2.0 compliance.

Cloud Hosting & Backups

Hosted on secure cloud infrastructure with automatic daily backups, high availability, and SSL encryption on all data in transit and at rest.
